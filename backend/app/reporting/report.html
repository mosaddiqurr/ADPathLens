Methodology and limitations

ADPathLens analyzes imported, authorized directory relationships using bounded traversal and deterministic risk rules. Confidence reflects imported evidence; priority reflects modeled relationship strength and path length.

A possible path is not evidence of compromise. Human review is required. Simulations do not change Active Directory. Removing a relationship may leave alternative routes; retest using fresh directory data.
